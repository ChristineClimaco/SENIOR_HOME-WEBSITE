Elderly Care

Our complimentary, comprehensive in-home consultation allows our professional staff to assess your family member's unique needs. From there, we develop a care plan with personalized solutions that improve safety and well-being.

Home Support Program

lderly Care provides personalized home-care services to seniors and individuals who need ongoing support while remaining in the comfort of their own homes. Our program is designed for Albertans who want flexibility, dignity, and greater choice in how their care is delivered.

To qualify for services:

  • You must reside in Alberta and have a valid Alberta Health Care card.
  • You must complete a home care assessment conducted by Alberta Health Services (AHS) or provide relevant medical documentation.
  • You must have a predictable and ongoing need for home-care support.
